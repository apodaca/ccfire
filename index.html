✅ Burn Safety Checklist

  • 📞 Call your local dispatch to notify them of your controlled burn.
  • 🧯 Ensure water and tools are on hand in case of an emergency escape.
  • 🚜 Create firebreaks by clearing dry vegetation around the burn area.
  • 👥 Never burn alone — always have help available.
  • 🌤️ Monitor the weather continuously, especially for sudden wind shifts or gusts.
  • 🔥 Stay with the fire until it is completely out and cold to the touch.
  • 📞 Call dispatch again when the burn is fully extinguished.

Understanding Fire Behavior & Weather

🌡️

Temperatures

Hot air pre-heats and dries out fuels, meaning they require less heat to ignite and burn faster.

🔻

Relative Humidity (RH)

When RH drops below 20%, fine fuels (grass, needles) dry rapidly, making them highly flammable.

💨

Winds & Gusts

Sustained wind drives fire spread, but sudden gusts easily cross firebreaks and cause spot fires miles away.

⚡

Dry Thunderstorms

Thunderstorms without rain produce abundant lightning (primary ignition) and erratic downdraft winds.
